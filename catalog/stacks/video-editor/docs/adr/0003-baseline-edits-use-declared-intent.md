# Baseline Edits Use Declared Intent

Status: accepted

VP-2 preserves source order while allowing deliberate removal of complete
filler, false starts, and repetition. Each accepted removal must declare its
source span, text, reason, and decision; transcript corrections remain a
non-editorial ASR-normalization overlay.

Editorial intent selects the exact proposed plan. Cut audit validates that
plan, and the same audited plan revision is compiled into the composition and
render. Verification compares the exact render with the corrected source
transcript minus declared removals, with no tolerance for unexplained omissions
or duplicated or clipped words.

This makes concise editing compatible with word-preservation proof, at the
cost of plan and render identity tracking plus explicit disposition of ASR
discrepancies. The current stack does not yet enforce a standard edit-intent
artifact, exact audit-to-plan lineage, or revision-bound verification evidence.

Sequence Optimization is intentionally outside this source-order baseline. If
used after baseline approval, it needs separate ordering/removal intent, an
exact audited and compiled plan, an Expected Transcript in the declared order,
identity-bound evidence, and its own human approval before enhancements or
motion.
